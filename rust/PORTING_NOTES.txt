Rust port of Wavlin, tracked module by module. The Kotlin sources stay untouched on this branch.

Ported (rust/crates, 47 unit and local-socket tests):
  lrclib   -> wavlin-lrclib
  kugou    -> wavlin-kugou
  lastfm   -> wavlin-lastfm
  deezer   -> wavlin-deezer
  ui-style -> wavlin-ui-style (pure parts of AppleGlass.kt, GlassEffect.kt, PlayerColorExtractor.kt; not wired into the app)

Not ported yet:
  innertube (11.8k lines), betterlyrics, paxsenix, shazamkit, metroproto, worker
  app (143k lines: Compose UI, Media3 playback, Room database, services, settings)

Known deviations are documented at the top of each crate's lib.rs.
Network paths were exercised only against local mock sockets, never against the live services.
